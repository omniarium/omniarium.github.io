my brain is a rabid dog, at times.

this idea came from a long forgotten project backlog, where i was borderline obsessed with web 1.0 aesthetic and personal blogs. for someone who likes writing from time to time and spend time working on random projects, this took long enough to finally come out.
at first, this was named "herbarium"; i love plants, green, moss (from the comfort of my home), but due to reasons, i opted for omniarium: i talk about lots of things, i have too much on my mind all the time... it reached a point that writing on a notepad and forgetting it elsewhere was being a type of mental clutter... which means this site is a home for every and anything inside my chaotic brain.
for a proper explanation on how my curious mind works, this had been sitting in the backlog for more than a year, and on a sudden burst of motivation and fear of normalcy, i decided to work on it. i saw lots of personal sites and blogs during this year and something of pseudo research, where i would be reading blogs and thinking about creating my own instead of actually creating it.

i spend absurd amounts of time inside my mind... it is the first omniarium i exist fully and without shame.
